### ✈️ Flying and Aircraft Access Rights

The club's fleet consists of the aircraft DA40NG (OH-STL) and DV20 (OH-IHQ) as well as a DA40 simulator. Club aircraft may only be used for private aviation (including refresher, training, and check flights). Commercial flying and activities similar to commercial flying are strictly prohibited.

- **Flying:** Using the club's aircraft requires a valid pilot's licence and a successfully completed club check ride with one of the club's instructors. The right to use the club's aircraft is available to junior and full members of the club who have been approved aircraft-specifically as equipment users.
- **Simulator:** Any member may book and use the simulator freely without a separate briefing.
- **Refresher flight:** A user whose engine experience is less than 150 hours and less than 20 hours on the type must complete a flight with a club instructor if more than six months have passed since the last flight.

  A fee of €10 may be charged for resolving missing or incorrect entries.

### 💡 Summary of Flight Operations Guidelines (for MIK aircraft)

- **Preparations:** Always check the electronic fault log in the aircraft information in the intranet before the flight. Carry out the external inspection of the aircraft in accordance with the aircraft flight manual (AFM).
- **Fuel:** Aircraft should not be fuelled more than is necessary for the safe completion of the flight. After a flight, it would be good to leave a maximum of: STL: 20 gal (approx. 75 l), IHQ: 15 gal (approx. 55 l) in the tanks. Take fuel samples before the first flight of the day (except in freezing conditions).
- **Nummela operations:** When operating at Nummela (EFNU), the Nummela Operations Manual, the field exam, and the PN requirement must be followed. The car must be parked on permitted asphalt-surfaced areas.
- **After the flight:** Clean insects from the aircraft's surfaces immediately with clean water and a pressure spray bottle. Move the aircraft to the hangar, place the wheel chocks and pitot cover in place. Leave the parking brake off in the hangar and keep the tow bar in place on the nose wheel.
- **Damage and defects:** Damage and defects must be reported immediately to the equipment manager. Do not contact the maintenance organisation yourself. If safety has been compromised, make a report in the club's SMS system (Safety Management System). In the event of an accident, the pilot in command is responsible for costs to the extent not covered by insurance (deductibles, etc.).
